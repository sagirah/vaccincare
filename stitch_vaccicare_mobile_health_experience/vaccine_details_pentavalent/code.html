Normal Body Response

Mild side effects are signs that your baby’s immune defense system is actively learning and building protective antibodies.

Common Mild Effects (1–2 Days)

thermostat
Mild Temperature / Fever

Warm forehead or low-grade fever. Keep baby in light cotton clothes and offer frequent breastfeeding.

radio_button_checked
Redness or Swelling at Thigh Site

Slight tenderness where the injection was given. A cool clean damp cloth helps relieve comfort.

mood_bad
Mild Irritability & Fussiness

Baby may sleep more or cry softly. Soothing maternal skin-to-skin touch provides immediate calm.

emergency

When to Visit Facility

If fever exceeds 39°C or crying lasts uninterrupted for more than 3 hours, immediately consult your local health worker or Murtala Mohammed Hospital.
